# Compare the edit with the original

- Does it preserve the meaning, facts, uncertainty and author's recognizable voice?
- Were any claims, numbers, quotes or opinions added without support?
- Does each cut remove actual repetition or filler rather than useful detail or personality?
- Are changes to structure or tone proportional to the requested edit?
- Does it read naturally without imposing a uniform rhythm or a list of banned words?

For detection, identify the passage and a concrete editing issue without rewriting or guessing authorship. Fix issues found in the comparison; do not require a separate evaluator or a score.
